# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GPA a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy, respect and constructive technical disagreement.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence and outcomes.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive material promptly.
- Accept responsibility, apologize and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information, credentials, recordings or screenshots
  without explicit permission.
- Deliberately unsafe automation examples or instructions that conceal impact.
- Sustained disruption of project or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainer may edit, remove or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled with discretion;
retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
